Keystone Assist Demo

Two demo apps for testing a read-only AI chat assistant with Virtuoso. Keystone CRM, its companies, people and figures are fictional sample data.

Keystone Assist WebsiteLanding page with the assistant pinned to the bottom centre. Use as appUrl. Keystone CRMA working demo CRM: sign-in, accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, meetings, calls and tasks, plus the Assist AI chat assistant. Use as crmUrl.

Knowledge base sources for Touchstone: CRM application KB · Assist chatbot KB · Touchstone directives (also linked inside the CRM, bottom left).

Demo controls: add #admin to either app link. Faults: #offtopic, #mutation, #noretrieval, #window, #empty, #slow, #relabel, #shadow (combine with dots, for example #admin.offtopic.slow).
